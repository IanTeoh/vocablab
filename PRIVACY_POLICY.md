# Privacy Policy for VocabLab

**Last updated:** [DATE]

VocabLab ("we," "our," or "the app") is a vocabulary-learning app. This
policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the
choices you have.

## Information We Collect

**Account information (optional).** Creating an account is never
required to use VocabLab — your learning progress is saved on your
device either way. If you choose to create an account, we collect:

- Email address and password (or, if you sign in with Google or
  Apple, the identity information those services provide)
- A username you choose
- An optional profile photo, if you upload one

**Gameplay and progress data.** Words, idioms, and roots you've
learned, streaks, achievement progress, and game high scores. If
you're signed in, a summary of this progress is stored in our
database so it can sync across your devices and be shown to friends
you add.

**Social data.** If you add friends, we store the friend connection
itself, and any friend requests you send or receive. If you report
another user, we store the report so it can be reviewed.

**We do not collect:** location data, contacts, browsing history
outside the app, or any data for advertising purposes. VocabLab does
not display ads.

## How We Use Information

- To provide the core app functionality — saving your progress,
  letting you add friends, and showing achievements and leaderboards
- To let your progress follow you across devices when you're signed in
- To respond to account support requests
- To review reports of abusive behavior between users

## Third-Party Services

VocabLab is built on **Firebase** (a Google Cloud service), which
provides:

- **Firebase Authentication** — manages sign-in and account security
- **Cloud Firestore** — stores your profile, progress, and friend data
- **Firebase Storage** — stores uploaded profile photos, if you choose to upload one

Firebase's own privacy practices are described at
[https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
We do not sell or share your data with any other third party.

## Your Choices

- **Account is optional.** You can use VocabLab entirely without
  creating one; your progress simply stays on your device.
- **You can edit your profile** (username, photo) at any time from
  Settings.
- **You can block another user**, which removes them as a friend and
  prevents them from contacting you again.
- **You can delete your account entirely** from Settings → Danger
  Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile,
  progress backup, and friend connections, and cannot be undone.

## Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists.
Deleting your account removes this data from our systems, aside from
report records already made against or by your account, which we
retain briefly for safety review purposes.

## Children's Privacy

VocabLab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you
believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us
at the address below and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at
the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take
effect means you accept the updated policy.
